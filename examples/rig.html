Original synthetic PMX rigs, VPD poses and VMD motions by mmd-gltf contributors; authored in this repository, no third-party assets. Both rigs retain BDEF1/2/4, duplicate exact names, forward parents, two materials and joint 259. The wedge has different geometry and hierarchy. Apply replaces all joints relative to original rest; omitted bones return to rest. Reapplication never accumulates offsets. Reset also works after loading a posed GLB with the reusable API.

Solver-free bone offsets only. SDEF/QDEF, deformation-layer ordering, IK/append, physics, morphs and non-bone VMD execution remain unsupported. VMD uses continuous destination-key curves, including adjacent frames; original-MMD equivalence of this project profile is unresolved. Applying VPD pauses motion; playing or seeking motion restores original-rest ownership. Converted files include approximate core TRS animation for ordinary loaders. A pose's parent filename and motion's model name are informational and never fetched.
