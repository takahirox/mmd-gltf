Original synthetic PMX rigs and VPD poses by mmd-gltf contributors; authored in this repository, no third-party assets. Both rigs retain BDEF1/2/4, duplicate exact names, forward parents, two materials and joint 259. The wedge has different geometry and hierarchy. Apply replaces all joints relative to original rest; omitted bones return to rest. Reapplication never accumulates offsets. Reset also works after loading a posed GLB with the reusable API.

Solver-free bone offsets only. SDEF/QDEF, deformation-layer ordering, IK/append, physics, morphs and VMD remain unsupported. Raw offsets do not reproduce full MMD poses. Original-MMD fidelity has not been measured. A pose's parent filename is informational and is never fetched.
